Refund policy

If a paid plan is not right for you, you have 30 days to get your money back.

Last updated 14 October 2026

In short

  • 30 days from your first payment, no hoops.
  • Free trials need no card, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Money goes back to the payment method you used.
  • We reply to refund requests within 2 working days.

01  The 30-day window

  1. Ask within 30 days of your first payment for a paid plan and we will refund it in full.
  2. The window starts on the date of the first payment and does not restart on later purchases of the same plan.

02  What is covered

ProductPlanRefund window
Laptop CarePro Annual, Pro Lifetime30 days from first payment
Nany SocialAny paid plan30 days from first payment

03  What is not covered

  1. Payments older than 30 days, unless the law where you live says otherwise.
  2. AI credits you have already used. Credits you used are deducted from any refund. Unused credits from a pack bought in the last 30 days can be refunded.
  3. Free plans and free trials, which cost nothing.

04  How to ask

  1. Email billing@nanybot.com from your account email.
  2. Include the product name and your payment or transaction ID. A reason is welcome but not required.
  3. We reply within 2 working days.

05  When the money comes back

  1. Refunds go to the original payment method, for example the PayPal account you paid with.
  2. Your payment provider usually posts it within 5 to 10 working days.

06  After a refund

  1. Paid features end and lock on your devices. Free features keep working.
  2. Your files, settings and backups are not touched.

07  Payment questions

  1. Anything unclear about a charge: billing@nanybot.com. See also the terms of service.
