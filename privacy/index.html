Privacy policy

What the Nanybot website collects, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated 14 October 2026

In short

  • This website has no ads, no ad trackers and no third-party analytics.
  • We never sell personal data.
  • Each software product has its own privacy policy for the data it handles.
  • You can ask for a copy or deletion of your data at any time.

01  Who we are

  1. Nanybot is a software service operated by Protim Soft (“we”, “us”).
  2. For privacy questions write to privacy@nanybot.com.

02  What this policy covers

  1. This policy covers nanybot.com and the messages you send us by email or through the contact page.
  2. It does not cover data handled inside our software. Laptop Care and Nany Social each publish a policy that describes what the product itself collects. Read that one before you install or sign up.

03  What we collect

  1. Messages you send. Your email address, name if you give it, and whatever you write to us.
  2. Server logs. When you load a page, our hosting provider records your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. This is standard for any website.
  3. Nothing else. The contact page does not send data from your browser to us. It opens your own email app with a message prepared for you to send.

04  Why we use it

  1. To answer your message and help with your problem.
  2. To keep the website secure and stop abuse, using the server logs.
  3. To meet legal obligations, such as tax or accounting records for paid purchases.

05  Who we share it with

  1. Our hosting provider and email provider process data on our behalf so the site and our inbox work.
  2. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.
  3. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

06  Cookies and fonts

  1. This site sets no cookies of its own.
  2. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When they do, Google receives your IP address and browser details in the font request.

07  How long we keep it

  1. Support emails are kept while we help you and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you write back.
  2. Server logs are kept for a short rolling period.
  3. Payment and tax records are kept for as long as the law requires.

08  Your choices

  1. You may ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.
  2. Email privacy@nanybot.com from the address you used with us. We aim to reply within 30 days.

09  Security and children

  1. The site is served over HTTPS and access to our inbox is limited to the people who need it.
  2. Our services are for business use and are not directed at children under 13.

10  Changes to this policy

  1. If we change this policy, the date at the top changes. For material changes we will also say so on the home page.
